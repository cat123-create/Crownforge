Terms of use

Last updated: November 23, 2026. By playing Crownforge you agree to these terms. If you are under 13, please read them with a parent or guardian.

1. Your account

  • Keep your username and password to yourself. You are responsible for what happens on your account.
  • One person can play one main account. Do not use extra accounts to send yourself items or to cheat.
  • Pick a kingdom name that is friendly. Names that are mean, rude or pretend to be someone else may be changed or banned.

2. Play fair

  • Do not cheat, hack, use bots, or use bugs or glitches to get items, resources or gems. If you find a bug, report it with the 🐞 Bug button.
  • Do not try to break, slow down or attack the game or its servers.
  • Do not trade game items for real money.

3. Be kind in chat

  • No bullying, hate, threats, rude words, scams or spam.
  • Never share personal information, like your real name, address, school, phone number, passwords or photos, and never ask others for theirs.
  • Do not ask people to meet in real life.
  • You can block, mute and report other players at any time.

4. Virtual items

Gold, stone, food, gems, crates, gear, pets, relics and every other item in Crownforge are part of the game. They have no real-money value, cannot be cashed out, and may be changed or rebalanced to keep the game fair and fun.

5. Ads

Crownforge may show ads from Google AdSense to help pay for the game. Ads are made by other companies. Do not click ads on purpose to help the game, and never ask others to. That breaks Google's rules. See the Privacy policy for how ads use cookies.

6. Bans and removal

The owner may warn, mute, reset or ban accounts that break these rules, and may remove names, messages or listings. Serious or repeated cheating can lead to a permanent ban.

7. The game can change

Crownforge is a free game run by one owner. Features may be added, changed or removed, and the game may sometimes be offline. The game is provided "as is", without any promises that it will always work perfectly.

8. Changes to these terms

If these terms change, this page and the date at the top will be updated. Questions? See the contact page.
