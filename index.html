Everyone who opens this page plays in the same world. Drag the map, pinch or scroll to zoom, and tap the minimap to jump anywhere. Buy land touching your borders, give your builders jobs, and tap your mines to collect. Train an army, open crates in the Shop for gear, build walls and hide traps, brew spells, and team up in a clan. Then raid barbarian camps and rival kingdoms for loot and trophies. Take a rival's Town Hall and you take everything they own.

      Settings

      You stay signed in and everything saves automatically, so the game opens straight into your kingdom. Use New account to start another kingdom, and Change account to switch between them.

      Your kingdom only makes gold and stone while you are online with the game open. While you are away it rests, but other players can still attack you, so build up your archers, walls, towers and traps before you log off.
