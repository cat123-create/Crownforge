Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Send a 🐞 bug report from inside the game, or see the contact page.

Is Crownforge free?

Yes. Everything can be earned by playing, and nothing is sold for real money. The game may show ads to help pay for running it.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Crownforge runs in your web browser on computers, tablets and phones. On a phone you can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.

How do I save my game?

Your kingdom saves automatically to your account. Log in with the same username and password on any device to keep playing.

I forgot my password. What can I do?

The game does not use email, so passwords cannot be reset by email. Send a 🐞 bug report from a device where you are still logged in, or write down your password somewhere safe.

Is it safe for kids?

Crownforge is made for all ages. Chat has a word filter, you can block, mute and report anyone, and the game never asks for real names, emails or addresses. Kids under 13 should ask a parent or guardian before playing.

Can other players attack me?

Yes, it is a strategy game. New kingdoms get a peace shield, you can build defenses, and you can make peace promises with other players.

What are gems for?

Gems are a rare reward. Use them for special crates, relics, switching job paths, resetting hero talents and other extras.

How do jobs work?

At Town Hall 5 you pick one of 20 jobs. Each job makes its own goods, has a special power, two paths to choose from and a mini-game. You can change jobs, but it costs resources and is limited.

What is a relic?

A rare magic item with a good side and a bad side. Wear up to three, and wear a full set for a bonus.

Why did my resources go up or down this week?

Each week has a world rule that changes the game, like more gold but less food. Check the 📅 World rule button to see this week's rule.

Someone is being mean. What do I do?

Tap their name, then block, mute or report them. Reports go to the owner, who can ban players who break the rules.

How do I report a bug or send an idea?

Use the 🐞 Bug button in the game. The owner reads every report.

Why do I see ads?

Ads from Google AdSense help pay for running the game. Crownforge asks Google to show only non-personalized ads. See the Privacy policy for details.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Send a 🐞 bug report from that account saying "please delete my account", and the owner will remove it.

How do I get stronger fast?

Keep your builders busy, collect resources often, raid barbarian camps, join a clan, and check the ✨ ready list on the home screen for things to do.
