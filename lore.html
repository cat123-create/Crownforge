📜 The story of Crownforge

Long before the first player founded a kingdom, the land of Crownforge was one great realm ruled by a single crown. This is the story of that realm, its places and its creatures, as the bards of Crownforge tell it.

📜 The Shattered Crown

The old kings ruled from a castle of gold at the center of the world. Their crown was forged from a falling star, and it was said that whoever wore it could make the land grow, the rivers run and the armies march as one. But the last king grew greedy. He tried to make the crown stronger by melting it with dragon fire, and the crown shattered.

Its pieces flew across the world. Where they landed, gold appeared in the hills, stone in the mountains and rich soil in the valleys. That is why every Gold Mine and Farm in Crownforge is said to hold a tiny spark of the old crown. And that is why so many young kingdoms rise up: each new ruler hopes to gather enough of that power to forge a crown of their own. That is how the land got its name: Crownforge.

🪓 The Barbarian Overlord

When the crown shattered, the barbarian tribes of the north saw their chance. A fierce warrior united them and called himself the Barbarian Overlord. His raiders built camps all over the land and attack any kingdom that looks weak. Twenty strongholds stand between the kingdoms and his throne:

Goblin Outpost → Muddy Ford → Wolfwood Camp → Bandit Mill → Crow Hill → Smugglers’ Cove → The Burnt Village → Old Stone Bridge → Raider Fort → Frostpine Pass → The Bone Yard → Iron Mine Hold → Troll Gate → Red River Camp → The Black Marsh → Warband Plains → Siege of Highwatch → Dragon’s Rest → The King’s Wall → Throne of the Barbarian Overlord.

Brave kings march through them one by one in the Campaign. On nights with a full moon, the barbarians go wild and attack twice as often, and the bards call them werewolf packs.

🗺️ Famous places

  • ⛰️ Crown Island rises out of the sea only at certain times. Kings race there to claim its treasure before the waves take it back.
  • ⛰️ King's Hill is the highest hill in the land. Legend says whoever holds it longest in a week is the true ruler, so clans fight for it every week.
  • 🌍 The Crown Wonder is a monument that the whole world builds together, stone by stone. Every month it is started again in honor of the old crown.
  • 🏰 The Great Fortress belongs to the barbarians and is far too strong for one kingdom. Clans gather to raid it together each week.
  • 🌋 The Volcano wakes up now and then, and kingdoms race to claim the land around it.
  • 🏛️ The Wonders: the old kings left plans for eight wonders: Great Tower, Golden Colossus, Sky Castle, Hanging Gardens, Grand Workshop, Great Lighthouse, Moon Temple, Crystal Spire. Only the richest kingdoms can build one.

🐉 Creatures of the realm

Great beasts roam the land, one each week: the Storm Griffin, the Shadow Wolf, the Golden Phoenix, the Frost Dragon and the Crystal Lion. Kings who find them and help defeat them earn great rewards. Smaller creatures live everywhere too. Goblins, trolls, giant spiders, skeletons, ghosts and golems guard the dungeons that kings build to test each other.

Not every creature is an enemy. Foxes, owls, wolves and even dragons hatch from eggs and become loyal pets that guard their kingdom. See the pet guide.

⚔️ The heroes

Twenty heroes have answered the call of the new kingdoms. Each one has a story and a special power:

  • Warlord: A mighty knight who slams the ground. Strong all-rounder. Their power, War Cry, gives +8% attack.
  • Arrow Queen: Fires a storm of arrows. The best hero at defending your kingdom. Their power, Arrow Storm, gives +10% attack.
  • Wizard: Hurls a huge fireball. The strongest attacker. Their power, Fireball, gives +12% attack.
  • Witch: Brews bubbling hexes that weaken whole armies. Their power, Hex Brew, gives +13% attack.
  • Viking Berserker: A wild warrior from the north who never stops swinging. Their power, Berserk Rage, gives +14% attack.
  • Paladin: A shining knight whose light protects every soldier. Their power, Holy Light, gives +15% attack.
  • Shadow Ninja: A silent fighter who strikes from the shadows. Their power, Shuriken Storm, gives +16% attack.
  • Druid: A forest guardian who makes roots and thorns burst from the ground. Their power, Thorn Wrath, gives +17% attack.
  • Dragon Knight: A knight in dragon-scale armor who breathes fire like a dragon. Their power, Dragon Fire, gives +18% attack.
  • Elf Ranger: A sharp-eyed elf whose arrows never miss. Their power, Hawk Volley, gives +19% attack.
  • Frost Queen: A queen of ice who freezes enemies in their tracks. Their power, Ice Storm, gives +20% attack.
  • Mountain King: A giant king of stone who shakes the ground with every step. Their power, Earthquake, gives +21% attack.
  • Samurai: A master swordsman with lightning-fast strikes. Their power, Blade Dance, gives +22% attack.
  • Necromancer: A dark mage who calls skeleton warriors to fight. Their power, Bone Army, gives +23% attack.
  • Valkyrie: A winged warrior who dives from the sky. Their power, Sky Charge, gives +24% attack.
  • Storm Sorcerer: A sorcerer who calls down lightning on his foes. Their power, Thunder Strike, gives +25% attack.
  • Beast Tamer: A wild hunter who fights alongside a pack of beasts. Their power, Wild Call, gives +26% attack.
  • Phoenix Knight: A blazing knight who rises again from the flames. Their power, Rebirth Flame, gives +27% attack.
  • Shadow King: A dark king whose shadow blots out the sun. Their power, Eclipse, gives +28% attack.
  • Eternal Emperor: The greatest hero of all, for rulers of Town Hall 100. Their power, Legend's Wrath, gives +29% attack.

Stats for every hero are in the hero guide.

🧑‍🌾 The twenty guilds

No kingdom can do everything alone. After the crown shattered, twenty guilds formed, from the Warriors and Merchants to the Alchemists and Bards. Each guild guards its own secrets, which is why only a Blacksmith can make a Whetstone and only a Wizard can write a Fire Scroll. When guilds share their goods, secret recipes can be discovered that no single guild knows. Read about every guild in the jobs guide.

👑 The Royal Council

Every ruler has a council of advisors who bring problems to the throne: wandering wizards, pirates with suspicious maps, baby dragons sleeping on the gold. Some say the old king lost his crown because he never listened to his council. Wise rulers listen, but they still have to choose.
