About Crownforge – a free online kingdom strategy game

Crownforge is a free multiplayer strategy game that you play in your web browser. There is nothing to download. Every player builds a kingdom in the same shared world, so the land next to yours belongs to a real person, and the barbarian camps, dragons and treasure on the map are there for everyone to find.

What you do in Crownforge

  • Build a kingdom. Start with a Town Hall and two builders. Buy land, then build Gold Mines, Stone Mines, Farms, Barracks, an Armory, walls, towers and traps.
  • Train an army. Soldiers, Archers, Giants, Cavalry, Catapults, Knights, Dragon Riders and more. Each troop is good at something different.
  • Fight and explore. Raid barbarian camps, take on bosses and dungeons, climb towers, and attack or defend against other players.
  • Collect heroes, pets, gear and relics. There are 20 heroes, pets you raise and train, dozens of weapons and armor pieces, and magic relics with good and bad sides.
  • Pick a job. Twenty jobs, from Blacksmith to Bard, each make their own special goods. Every job has two paths to choose from, a mini-game, and secret recipes to discover.
  • Play together. Join a clan, raid a fortress as a team, fight for King's Hill, trade at the markets, build world projects, race and duel other players live, and make peace or trade deals with other kings.
  • Make choices. The Royal Council asks you to decide what your kingdom does, and every week a new world rule changes how the game plays.

Who makes Crownforge

Crownforge is an independent, fan-made game built and run by one owner, not by a big company. New features are added often, many of them suggested by players. If you find a problem or have an idea, use the 🐞 Bug button inside the game. The owner reads every report.

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything in Crownforge can be earned by playing. The game does not sell anything for real money. To help pay for running the game, Crownforge may show ads from Google AdSense. You can read more about ads and cookies in the Privacy policy.

Keeping players safe

  • Chat has a word filter, and any player or message can be reported with one tap.
  • You can block or mute anyone. Blocked players cannot message you or challenge you to duels.
  • The game never asks for your real name, email, address or phone number.
  • Read the rules and the privacy policy to learn more.
