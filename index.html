👑 Crownforge

Crownforge is a free multiplayer kingdom strategy game you play right in your browser. Build a castle, train an army, raise pets, pick a job and battle barbarians and other players, all in one shared world. No download, no payments.

▶️ Play now, it's free 📖 How to play

What you can do

Build your kingdom

Buy land, build mines, farms, barracks, walls and towers, and grow from a village into a mighty kingdom.

Open crates and collect gear

16 kinds of crates and 36 weapons, armor pieces, shields and tools. Every crate shows its odds.

Raise pets

12 pets that guard your kingdom, learn tricks, go on adventures and evolve.

Pick one of 20 jobs

Every job makes its own goods. Choose a path, play its mini-game and discover secret recipes.

Make royal decisions

The Royal Council brings you a new problem every few hours. Will you take the gamble?

So much to do

Duels, races, clan raids, King's Hill, dungeons, bosses, auctions, world projects and more.

How it works

  1. Make an account with a username and password. No email needed.
  2. Found your kingdom. You start with a Town Hall, two builders and a peace shield.
  3. Grow! Build, raid barbarian camps, join a clan and climb the leaderboards. Read the beginner guide for tips.
