Pet tips

  • Feed and play with your pet to make it happy. A happy pet gets +5% guard and pet power for 8 hours.
  • Send pets on adventures to bring back gold, gems and a story.
  • Pet Festival week makes every pet much stronger.
  • Try Pet Battles and Pet Visits with friends.
