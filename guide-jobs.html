All 20 Crownforge jobs

← All guides · Made from the game data of version 151.

At Town Hall 5 you can pick a job. Each job has its own power and is the only maker of its goods. Other players buy your goods at the Job Market, so every job matters. At job level 2 you choose one of two paths, and at job level 4 your path power gets 1.5× stronger.

⚔️ Warrior

+10% attack. 📯 Battle Cry: once every 8 hours, your attacks are 25% stronger for 10 minutes. 🎯 Drill Yard: free soldiers once a day. Only Warriors make 🛡️ Bodyguard Contracts (+15% defense for 4 hours, and it blocks Spy sabotage).

Level up by: battles won, plus making goods.

Goods only Warriors can make

  • 🛡️ Bodyguard Contract (job level 1): +15% defense for 6 hours. Spies cannot sabotage you while it is on.
  • 🚩 War Banner (job level 2): +15% attack for 3 hours.
  • 🏰 Fortify Kit (job level 3): +25% defense, blocks Spy sabotage for 4.5 hours.
  • 📯 Champion's Horn (job level 4): +20% attack, +15% fewer troop losses for 4.5 hours.

Paths (choose one at job level 2)

  • 🪓 Berserker: +8% attack. Special item: 🧪 Rage Potion (+25% attack, -10% defense for 3 hours.)
  • 🛡️ Guardian: +8% defense. Special item: 🛡️ Tower Shield (+25% defense, 10% fewer troop losses for 3 hours.)
