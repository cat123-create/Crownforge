🧭 Strategy tips

These tips help you grow faster and win more battles. They work for new players and for experienced kings.

🏗️ Building and resources

  • Never let a builder sit still. Start a long upgrade before you log off.
  • Mines and farms first. More income makes everything else faster. Decorations can wait.
  • Collect often so you can spend your resources sooner. The collect-all button saves time.
  • Upgrade your Town Hall at the right time. It unlocks new buildings and troops, but finish your important upgrades first.
  • Build Farms before a big army. Training troops costs food.

⚔️ Battles

  • Raid barbarian camps close to your level. They are the safest way to get loot.
  • Use the right troops. Wall Smashers, Rams and Catapults smash defenses, and Giants take hits. In the clan fortress raid and player dungeons, troops that hit a weak spot do double damage.
  • Check before you attack a player. If their defense is much higher than your attack, you will lose troops for nothing.
  • Use your shield wisely. While it is on, build walls, towers and traps.
  • Get revenge on players who attack you. Your reports show who it was.

🧑‍🌾 Jobs and the economy

  • Pick a job that fits how you play. Fighters like Warrior or Hunter, builders like Builder or Miner, and traders like Merchant or Banker.
  • Play the mini-game well when you make goods. A perfect score gives 2 extra goods.
  • Sell what you make. Other players need your goods for their buffs, recipes and Royal Commissions.
  • Experiment with recipes. Mixing goods from different jobs can unlock powerful secret items. Use the hints in your recipe book.
  • Watch the 3% sales tax. It only applies from Town Hall 5, so plan big sales.

📅 Plan around the weekly world rule

Every week has a rule that changes the game. The game shows you next week's rule too, so you can plan ahead. The rules are:

  • ⚔️ Week of War: Attacks are stronger, but buildings make less. (+25% attack, -10% from all buildings)
  • 🏰 Week of Walls: Defenses are stronger, but attacks are weaker. (+25% defense, -10% attack)
  • 💰 Golden Week: Lots more gold, but less food. (+30% gold, -15% food)
  • 🌾 Harvest Week: Food and gardens boom, but loot is smaller. (+40% food, +30% faster garden, -10% loot)
  • 🏗️ Builder's Week: Everything builds faster, but attacks are a bit weaker. (30% faster building, -5% attack)
  • 🐉 Monster Week: Bosses and camps are easier to beat, but defenses are weaker. (+40% boss damage, +20% attack vs camps, -10% defense)
  • 🐾 Pet Festival: Pets are much stronger all week! (+40% pet power)
  • 🩸 Brutal Week: Attacks hit harder, but you lose more troops. (+15% attack, 20% more troop losses)
  • 🪨 Stone Age Week: Lots more stone, but less gold. (+40% stone, -10% gold)
  • 💎 Treasure Week: Bigger loot from everything, but buildings make less. (+30% loot, +15% attack vs camps, -10% from all buildings)

For example, save your big attacks for Week of War, and do your big building projects in Builder's Week.

🔮 Relics and talents

  • Every relic has a downside. Pick relics whose bad side does not matter for your play style.
  • Go for a full set. Three relics from the same set give a strong bonus.
  • Hero talents can't be stacked. In each row you get one of two. Fighters like attack talents, and builders like Steward and Royal Engineer.

🤝 Playing with others

  • Join an active clan. Clanmates donate troops, and you can raid the clan fortress together and fight for King's Hill.
  • Make trade and defense pacts with friendly neighbors. Never break a peace promise, because everyone will see it.
  • Help with Royal Commissions and the World Project. Everyone who helps gets the reward.

🛡️ Stay safe

  • Never share your password, real name, address or school.
  • Block, mute or report anyone who is mean.
