How to play Crownforge – beginner guide and tips

This guide explains everything you need to know to grow from a tiny village into a mighty kingdom. You can read it from top to bottom, or jump to a section.

1. Getting started

Open the game, make an account with a username and password, and give your kingdom a name. You start with a Town Hall, two builders and a peace shield that protects you from other players for your first two hours. A short tutorial shows you the basic buttons.

Your Town Hall is the heart of your kingdom. Upgrading it unlocks new buildings, troops, heroes and features, so plan your upgrades around it. If your kingdom ever falls, you can rebuild somewhere new and keep your gems, trophies, pets and heroes.

2. Resources

  • 💰 Gold pays for most buildings, troops and upgrades. Gold Mines make it.
  • 🪨 Stone is needed for stronger buildings and walls. Stone Mines and Quarries make it.
  • 🍖 Food feeds your army. Training troops costs food, so build Farms early.
  • 💎 Gems are rare. You get them from achievements, quests, events, bosses and some rewards. Spend them on special crates, relics and extras.

Buildings fill up over time. Tap them to collect, or use the collect-all button. Different jobs, relics, decrees and weekly world rules can boost how much you make.

3. Buildings

To build, you first need land. Buy empty land next to your kingdom, then pick what to build on it. Important buildings include:

  • Gold Mine, Stone Mine, Quarry and Farm make your resources.
  • Barracks train troops. Upgraded Barracks unlock stronger troops.
  • Armory sells gear each day, and higher levels sell rarer gear.
  • Laboratory researches upgrades for your troops.
  • Walls, Guard Towers, Cannons and Spike Traps protect you from attackers.
  • Dock, Road and Bridge help you reach and connect more of the map.

Builders can only work on one thing at a time, so keep them busy. Upgrades take longer as levels go up.

4. Troops

Train troops in your Barracks. Each one has a job:

  • Soldiers are cheap and reliable. Archers stay home and defend.
  • Giants are tough and lose fewer troops. Cavalry are fast and strong.
  • Catapults, Wall Smashers and Battering Rams smash through defenses.
  • Knights and Dragon Riders are powerful late-game troops. Healers keep your army alive.

Your army size is limited by your buildings and Town Hall. Some fights, like dungeons and the clan fortress, have weak spots, so bringing the right troop type can double your damage.

5. Fighting

Barbarian camps appear around the map. Raid them for gold, stone and sometimes gems. Bigger camps hit harder but give more.

Attacking players: tap an enemy building, choose your troops and watch the battle. Winning gives loot and trophies. Losing costs troops, so check their defense first. Players with a shield cannot be attacked.

Defending: walls, towers, traps, archers, heroes and pets all help. You can get revenge on players who attack you.

There is much more to fight: the Daily Boss, Boss Rush, World Boss, dungeons, Crown Island, the tower climb, player-built dungeons and live 1v1 duels.

6. Heroes and talents

There are 20 heroes, from the Warlord to the Eternal Emperor. Recruit heroes, upgrade them, and put the best ones in your battle team. Each hero has a special power that boosts your attack.

For every 5 hero levels you have in total, you earn a talent point. Every 2 points opens a new row in the talent tree. In each row you pick one of two talents, like more loot or stronger camp attacks. You cannot have both, so choose the one that fits how you play.

7. Pets

Pets hatch from eggs and Pet Crates. They guard your kingdom, grow stronger as you level them up, and can learn tricks. Two of the same pet can be merged into a stronger one. Try Pet Battles and Pet Visits with friends.

8. Crates, gear and relics

Crates hold resources, troops and gear. Some are bought with gold, stone or food, like the Wooden Crate, Explorer's Pack, Lucky Crate or Dragon's Hoard. Others cost gems, like the Royal Crate. Every crate shows its odds before you open it.

Gear comes in four rarities: Common, Rare, Epic and Legendary. Equip a weapon, armor, shield and tool to boost attack, defense, losses, mining or building speed. Two matching items can be forged into a better one.

Relics are rare magic items with a good side and a bad side. A Dragon Fang gives +20% attack but −8% defense, for example. Wear up to three, and wear a full set of three for a set bonus.

9. Jobs, paths and recipes

At Town Hall 5 you can choose one of 20 jobs, like Warrior, Merchant, Farmer, Blacksmith, Wizard or Bard. Each job has a special power and is the only maker of its own goods, which other players can buy from you.

Job levels unlock better goods. At job level 2 you choose one of two paths. A Warrior becomes a Berserker or a Guardian, for example. Each path has its own power and its own special item.

Making goods starts a short mini-game that fits your job, like timing hammer hits, catching crops, copying a pattern or counting coins. Do well to make extra goods.

Secret recipes: mix two or three goods from different jobs to discover hidden items. The recipe book gives you a hint for each one you have not found yet.

10. Clans

Join or create a clan to chat, donate troops, finish clan quests and raid a giant clan fortress together each week. The live raid feed shows every hit your clanmates make. Clans also battle in Clan Wars and fight to hold King's Hill. The clan that holds the hill longest in a week wins gems and a Royal Crate for everyone who fought.

11. Playing with others

  • Live duels: challenge someone who is online. You both secretly pick Strike, Block or Power each round. The first to win two rounds wins.
  • Races: a 15-second tapping race against other players.
  • World project: everyone donates to build the Crown Wonder together, and every stage gives rewards.
  • Royal Commissions: big weekly projects that need goods from several jobs.
  • Friends: add friends, message them and get a pop-up when they come online.

12. Council, world rules and deals

Every few hours the Royal Council brings you a problem, like a wizard asking to stay the night or pirates selling a map. Your choice can give rewards, a temporary decree or a surprise. Some choices are a gamble.

Every week a new world rule changes the whole game. During Week of War attacks are stronger but buildings make less, and Harvest Week brings lots of food. The game also shows you next week's rule so you can plan.

Kingdom deals let you make peace promises, trade pacts or defense pacts with other players. Both kings must agree. If you break a peace promise by attacking, everyone sees you as a promise breaker for three days.

13. Markets and trading

Sell crates, gear and resources at the Market. Job goods are sold at the Job Market, and there are fast 2-minute hot auctions and longer gem auctions. A small 3% sales tax applies to big sales (not below Town Hall 5). You can also post orders asking other players for things you need.

14. Tips for new players

  1. Build Farms early. An army with no food cannot train.
  2. Keep both builders busy, and upgrade mines before decorations.
  3. Raid small barbarian camps before attacking players.
  4. Use your peace shield to grow, and build walls and towers before it runs out.
  5. Join a clan as soon as you can. Clanmates donate troops and help with raids.
  6. Check the ✨ ready list on the home screen. It tells you what you can do right now.
  7. Never share your password or personal information with anyone in the game.
