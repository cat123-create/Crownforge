👑 Crownforge

Crownforge is a free multiplayer kingdom strategy game you play right in your browser. Build a castle, train an army, raise pets, pick a job and battle barbarians and other players, all in one shared world.

  • ✅ 100% free, nothing to buy
  • ✅ No download, works on phones and computers
  • ✅ Play with friends and clans

What you can do

Build your kingdom

Buy land, build mines, farms, barracks, walls and towers, and grow from a village into a mighty kingdom.

Open crates and collect gear

16 kinds of crates and 36 weapons, armor pieces, shields and tools. Every crate shows its odds.

Pick one of 20 jobs

Every job makes its own goods. Choose a path, play its mini-game and discover secret recipes.

Play board games

Tic-tac-toe, connect four and checkers against real players, with an optional gold bet.

Rule the seas

Build warships and battle pirates, the Sea Serpent, the Kraken and other fleets.

Raise pets and win the pet show

12 pets that guard your kingdom. Enter your best one in the weekly pet show!

📱 Play anywhere

Crownforge runs in any modern web browser on phones, tablets and computers. Log in with the same username on any device and your kingdom is right there. On a phone, you can add Crownforge to your home screen so it opens like an app.

How it works

  1. Make an account with a username and password. No email needed.
  2. Found your kingdom. You start with a Town Hall, two builders and a peace shield.

❓ Quick questions

Is Crownforge really free?

Yes. Everything can be earned by playing, and nothing is sold for real money.

Is it safe for kids?

Chat has a word filter, every player can block, mute and report others, and the game never asks for your real name, email or address. Kids under 13 should ask a parent first.

Do I need to download anything?

No, it runs in your browser. On phones you can add it to your home screen.
