🔒 Privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026. This explains, in plain words, what the Crownforge game saves and why.

👑 Who runs Crownforge

Crownforge is a free fan-made game run by its owner, not by a company. If you have a question about privacy or want your data removed, use the 🐞 Bug button in the game (see the contact page).

📝 What we collect

  • Your username and password. We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number or address. Your password is checked by Google Firebase, and the owner can never see it.
  • Your game: your kingdom, land, buildings, troops, pets, items, clan, friends, trophies and other progress.
  • Things you type: your kingdom name, clan chat, private messages, notes on gifts and orders, map notes, poll votes, and bug reports.
  • Bug reports also include the game version, your screen size, whether you are on a phone or computer, and your browser name, so problems can be fixed.
  • Player reports: if someone reports you, a few of your recent chat messages are sent with the report.
  • Feature counts: the game counts which features get used (like "Dungeon: 3 times") so the owner knows what players like. These counts are saved with your game.
  • On your device: your settings (like sound, language and graphics) are saved in your browser so they stay the same next time.

👀 Who can see it

  • Other players can see your kingdom name, your kingdom on the map, your profile, your clan chat messages and things you list for sale.
  • Private messages and group chats only show up for the people in that chat. But they are not secret like a diary: they are stored in the game's data, and the owner can see them if there is a problem. Never share private information in messages.
  • The owner can see game data, reports and bug reports to keep the game fair and safe.

🔧 Other services the game uses

  • Google Firebase stores accounts and game data.
  • Google Fonts and Google's code servers send the game its lettering and code. Like any website, they can see your internet address (IP address).
  • Google Translate is only loaded if you turn on translation in Settings.
  • Google AdSense shows ads (see Ads below).
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.

📢 Ads and cookies

  • Crownforge shows ads from Google AdSense to help pay for the game. Ads are made by other companies, not by Crownforge.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (small files in your browser) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Because many players are kids, Crownforge asks Google to show only non-personalized ads to everyone. Non-personalized ads are based on things like the page and your general location, not on your browsing history. Google may still use cookies to limit how often an ad is shown and to fight fraud.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland may see a consent message from Google before ads are shown.

🚫 What we never do

  • We never sell your information or give it to anyone to sell things to you.
  • Crownforge itself does not use tracking cookies. Only ad partners like Google may, as explained above.

🧒 Kids

Crownforge is for players of all ages. If you are under 13, ask a parent or guardian before you make an account. Never share your real name, school, address, phone number, passwords or photos of yourself in chat or messages, even with friends from the game. Parents can ask for a child's account to be deleted at any time (see below).

🛡️ Staying safe

You can 🚫 block or 🔇 mute anyone, ⚠️ report a player or a mean message, and see who you blocked in ⚙️ Settings. Reports go to the owner. Read the rules too.

🗑️ Keeping and deleting your data

Your game is kept as long as the game is running, so you can come back to it. To have your account and kingdom deleted, send a 🐞 bug report that says "please delete my account" from that account, and the owner will remove it.

🔄 Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the date at the top will be updated.
