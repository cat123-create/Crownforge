Contact

Crownforge is run by one owner. The fastest way to reach them is from inside the game, so your message comes with the information needed to help you.

🐞 Bugs, ideas and questions

  1. Open the game and log in.
  2. Tap the 🐞 Bug button.
  3. Write what happened or what you would like, and send it. The owner reads every report and can reply to you in the game.

⚠️ Reporting a player

Tap the player's name, then tap ⚠️ Report. Their recent chat is sent with the report so the owner can see what happened. You can also block or mute them right away.

🔒 Privacy and deleting your account

To ask about your data or to have your account deleted, send a 🐞 bug report from that account. To delete it, write "please delete my account". See the Privacy policy for details.

👪 Parents and guardians

If you have a question or concern about your child's account, log in to the game with your child, send a 🐞 bug report, and say that you are the parent. The owner will follow up in the game.
